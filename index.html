About Blake

Blake Smith is running for Waterloo City Council in Ward 4 because every part of Waterloo deserves attention, investment, and representation.

Having grown up on both the East and West Sides of Waterloo, Blake has personally seen the unequal quality and investment between the two sides of the city.

Today, Blake lives on the East Side and believes the East Side deserves leadership that fights for its neighborhoods, roads, businesses, recreation, and future.

This campaign is built around one idea: not running for politics — running for YOU.

Key Issues

Lower Taxes

Waterloo residents face some of the highest taxes in Iowa. Blake supports cutting wasteful spending, improving efficiency, and responsibly lowering the burden on taxpayers, especially property taxes.

Fix Roads Properly

Road repairs should last. Blake supports affordable, durable repair solutions instead of repeated temporary fixes, along with repainting faded road lines and improving safety.

Support the East Side

The East Side deserves equal attention and investment. Blake supports improving parks, recreation, housing, beautification, and economic opportunity throughout Ward 4.

Community Investment

Blake opposes decisions that weaken East Side recreation and identity, including choices like replacing Gate's Pool with a cement splash pad instead of preserving a stronger community attraction.
